Frequently Asked Questions About Contacting The Indian Harbor

How do I contact The Indian Harbor?

You can contact The Indian Harbor by calling (754) 200-4891 or emailing info@theindianharbor.com. You can also submit the inquiry form on this page, and our team will respond promptly to discuss your event and provide a customized catering proposal.

Where is The Indian Harbor located?

The Indian Harbor is located at 1515 SE 17th St Suite No 102, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33316. We serve clients throughout Florida including Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Boca Raton, Palm Beach, Orlando, Tampa, and Jacksonville.

What areas does The Indian Harbor serve?

The Indian Harbor serves Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Boca Raton, Palm Beach, Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville, Miami Beach, Hollywood, Weston, Coral Springs, Aventura, and Pompano Beach. We are based at 1515 SE 17th St Suite No 102, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33316 and are available for events statewide.

How quickly does The Indian Harbor respond to inquiries?

The Indian Harbor responds to most catering inquiries within one business day. For urgent or last-minute events, call us directly at (754) 200-4891 and our team will help check availability for your date right away.

How far in advance should I book The Indian Harbor?

We recommend booking The Indian Harbor at least 4 to 8 weeks in advance for most events, and earlier for large weddings or multi-day celebrations. We can often accommodate shorter timelines based on availability, so call (754) 200-4891 to check your date.

How do I get a catering quote from The Indian Harbor?

To get a catering quote, submit the inquiry form on this page with your event type, date, and guest count, or call (754) 200-4891. We will prepare a customized proposal and can arrange a complimentary tasting before you book.

What information should I include in my catering inquiry?

Please include your event type, event date, estimated guest count, location, and any cuisine or dietary preferences. The more detail you provide, the faster The Indian Harbor can tailor a menu and quote for your event.

What payment methods does The Indian Harbor accept?

The Indian Harbor accepts cash, credit cards, debit cards, and checks. Our team will explain deposit and payment details when preparing your customized catering proposal. Call (754) 200-4891 or email info@theindianharbor.com for more information.
