About The Indian Harbor

What Kind of Business Is The Indian Harbor?

The Indian Harbor is a premier catering and event-planning company based at 1515 SE 17th St Suite No 102, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33316, serving Fort Lauderdale, Miami, and all of South Florida. We cater weddings, corporate events, and private celebrations with customizable Indian, fusion, and international menus. Call (754) 200-4891 or email info@theindianharbor.com to plan your event.

For over a decade, The Indian Harbor has been Florida's most trusted name in catering and event planning. What started as a passion for bringing people together through exceptional food has grown into the premier hospitality group serving South Florida's most prestigious events.

We specialize in creating unforgettable experiences for weddings, corporate events, and private celebrations. Our team of culinary experts and event planners work tirelessly to ensure every detail exceeds your expectations, from the first consultation to the final cleanup. Explore our full catering services or contact The Indian Harbor to start planning.
